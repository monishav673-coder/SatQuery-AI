    About SatQuery AI & Scientific Architecture

    Autonomous Vision-Language Assistant for Multimodal Remote Sensing Image Analysis.

    1. Agentic AI Pipeline

    SatQuery AI decouples high-level natural language user queries from underlying complex remote-sensing computer vision models. The Agent Controller decomposes input queries into directed sub-tasks, dispatches work to specialized analytical pipelines, calculates dynamic multi-factor confidence metrics, and synthesizes evidence-grounded interpretations.

    2. Specialist Remote Sensing Suite

    • Building Detection: Structural edge-gradient analysis and spectral roof reflectance segmentation with 9-quadrant compass orientation.
    • Water Body Segmentation: RGB-NDWI proxy and HSV cyan-blue envelope extraction with morphological boundary isolation.
    • Agriculture & Vegetation Index: Visible Atmospherically Resistant Index (VARI) & Excess Green (ExG) canopy density measurement.
    • BigEarthNet Land Cover Classification: Corine-compatible 5-class remote sensing land-cover mapping.
    • Multitemporal Differential Change Specialist: Sub-pixel ORB co-registration and Delta-E radiometric difference analysis.
    • Optical-SAR Feature Fusion: Cross-validates optical surface reflectance with microwave radar backscatter intensity.

    3. Scientific Integrity & Honesty

    SatQuery AI strictly calculates all building counts, water areas, agricultural canopy percentages, and change metrics directly from actual pixel arrays. In the absence of calibrated Ground Sample Distance (GSD) or sensor GCPs, measurements are transparently designated as image-relative percentages.
